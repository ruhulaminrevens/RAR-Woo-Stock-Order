Mobile-first WooCommerce staff PWA for controlled stock updates and fast manual/social order creation.

== Description ==

RAR Woo Stock & Order adds a private staff web app (default /staff/) designed for phone-first stock management and manual/social order entry.

Features:
* Staff PWA dashboard with Today's Orders, Today's Sales, Low Stock and Out of Stock.
* Stock Manager with item/SKU search, image, current price, stock quantity and one-tap stock update.
* Create Order workflow for customer details, items, quantity, price, shipping and notes.
* Order-line price override can be enabled/disabled by admin.
* Safe role: Woo Stock & Order Staff can update stock and create orders but cannot add/delete products by default.
* Optional simple-product creation and Trash-only deletion controlled by admin.
* WooCommerce-native orders, stock reduction and status/email hooks.
* HPOS compatibility.
* Nonce/capability protected writes.
* Installable PWA shell.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the installable ZIP in WordPress > Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin.
2. Activate RAR Woo Stock & Order.
3. Open WooCommerce > Stock & Order.
4. Configure permissions and default order status.
5. Assign staff users the role "Woo Stock & Order Staff".
6. Open /staff/ on the phone and use Chrome > Add to Home Screen.
